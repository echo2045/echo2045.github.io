# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Broad personal-brand audience: recruiters and studios evaluating Nafis, players and community discovering his games, and esports/industry peers checking credentials. Every audience arrives at the same single page, so proof must be legible to all of them at a glance.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for **Nafis Forkan**, game developer (gameplay programmer, Unity, Unreal Engine, game systems) and esports community leader in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The page exists to make his real track record legible: shipped games at scale, competitive-esports operations, and published research. Success means a visitor leaves believing the numbers and knowing how to reach him.

## Positioning

Not an aspirational portfolio — a shipped-record portfolio. The differentiator is verifiable scale: 10M+ downloads on a live title, an IEEE-published serious game, and national-level esports operations. Few portfolio sites can truthfully claim either side of that pair (game dev + esports ops).

## Operating Context

Static single-page site on GitHub Pages (echo2045.github.io), built with Vite + React, deployed by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` on push to `main`. Read on phones and desktops alike; visitors likely arrive from GitHub, LinkedIn, or a shared link.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Vite + React single-page app; no backend, no router, no CMS.
- No real imagery on hand — project cards must not fabricate screenshots; art-directed CSS/SVG is acceptable, invented screenshots are not.
- Outbound links: GitHub (github.com/echo2045), LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/nafis-forkan-b24922184), Play Store pages for Bus Simulator Bangladesh (com.GhostInteractive.BusSimulatorBangladesh) and Endless Dhaka (com.GhostInteractive.EndlessDhaka / iOS id1598554070), IEEE Xplore for Stinger (ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11313522), plus his own repos as side quests (Speak-and-Play, Retsnom.Inc, BCGameJam2020-Polar-Bear-Run, Task1---Multiplayer-Race under github.com/echo2045).
- **Open decision:** contact email — none confirmed; a single `LINKS` constant in `src/main.jsx` holds it for him to fill in.
- **Open decision:** Truck Simulator Bangladesh store URL — unconfirmed; link stays off until he supplies it.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Nafis Forkan", self-description "Game Developer", disciplines: gameplay programming, Unity, Unreal Engine, game systems. Confirmed facts only: 10M+ downloads scale, ~1M new downloads contributed, 1 IEEE paper (Stinger, SNPD 2025), 3 live + 2 upcoming games, 10+ online and 5+ LAN tournaments, 500+ players supported, 24+ teams managed, 2 national qualifiers, national-team participation in international events.

## Evidence on Hand

The numbers above, three named shipped projects (Bus Simulator Bangladesh, Endless Dhaka, Truck Simulator Bangladesh), employer Ghost Interactive, and the published paper. No testimonials, no screenshots, no press assets — future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Numbers are the hero: lead with verifiable scale, not adjectives.
- Every claim must be checkable — links go to real stores and profiles, never dead anchors.
- One page, zero friction: no navigation burden, everything scannable.
- Craft signals competence: for a gameplay programmer, the site's own polish is portfolio evidence.
